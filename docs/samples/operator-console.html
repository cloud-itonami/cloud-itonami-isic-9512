Approved & committed path — and what the store keeps of it

Measured, not asserted: for every committed record this run, the approver is looked for at three points — granted by the human on resume, handed to the store on the record's :payload, and read back out of the SSoT through the Store protocol afterwards. 6 human approvals were granted; 4 survive in the SSoT; 2 were dropped; and 0 are recorded in the persisted audit ledger. This is a real defect in this actor, derived here rather than described: commrepair.store's own docstring promises the ledger answers "approved by whom", but commit-record! drops the approver for mark-completed, mark-returned (it drafts the registry record from :path and never reads :payload), and the :approval-granted fact commrepair.operation emits stays in the run's :audit channel — only :commit and :hold write to store/ledger. These counts are computed at render time, so this notice disappears on its own once the store retains the approver.
